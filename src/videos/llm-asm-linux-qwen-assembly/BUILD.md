I gated the s5 benchmark and s6 prefill panels to their own scenes (they were bleeding into s6/s7 and overlapping), so s6 now shows only the GEMV vs GEMM lines, s7 shows only the hardware requirements, and s8/s9/s11 show their install, weight-export/build and save/follow content; the Cloudcode lockup now leaves with the opening hook.
