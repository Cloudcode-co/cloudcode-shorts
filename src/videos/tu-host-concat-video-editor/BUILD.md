An engineer's-notebook video in four long shots. Each one is a squared-paper page (the opening page is dark navy to carry the Cloudcode lockup) that fills up in place while the voice runs. Boxes, arrows, doodles and the checklist ticks draw themselves on with stroke animation, key words get highlighter swipes, and the Concat avatar, Claude, Rust, FFmpeg, GitHub and OS icons are taped in on paper tiles. Sticky notes carry the asides, and the `cargo run` commands type in the kit Terminal.

Shots: A covers the hook, what Concat is and "100% local"; B covers offline AI and the editor toolkit; C covers the MCP/JSON-RPC diagram and the build command; D covers installs and the sign-off. The type is Patrick Hand, which has Vietnamese support. The palette is ink navy, highlighter yellow and red pen on blue-grid paper, with the avatar's gold echoed in the highlighter. The only number on screen is the star count from facts.json.

`npm run register` and `npx tsc` pass. I looked at the QA stills from every shot over three rounds and fixed an early-appearing tape, an element that touched the TikTok button column, and a sticky-note line break. The rendered video and audio were not played back.
