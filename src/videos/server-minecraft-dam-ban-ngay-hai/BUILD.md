# Build: server-minecraft-dam-ban-ngay-hai

Built a Vietnamese TikTok meme skit short for "Lời nguyền mở server Minecraft cho đám bạn", set against a gaming PC setup still background with subtle beat-reactive pumping. The pacing follows four chapter title cards introducing each section ("Kèo thề sống chết", "Đêm đầu huy hoàng", "Đúng 24 giờ sau...", "Lời nguyền server 2 ngày"), followed by quick cuts to big bold yellow outlined MemeCaptions at the top and animated meme animals (thumbs-up cats, fast typing cat, dancing cats, wide-eyed screaming cat on the 0/20 drop, betrayed tabby cat, and crying cat with crying dog) in the lower half. Fixed right-zone overlay clearance for chapter titles and sticker bounds during QA inspection, replaced stickers containing promotional watermark text with clean expressive animated memes, and synced flash transitions and sound effects to cut boundaries.
