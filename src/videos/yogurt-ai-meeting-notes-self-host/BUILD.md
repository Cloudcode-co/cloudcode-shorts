# Video Build: yogurt-ai-meeting-notes-self-host

Built a documentary-style TikTok explainer uncovering the misconception of renting cloud VPS to host meeting AI note-taking tools, highlighting Yogurt (Electron, local whisper.cpp & llama.cpp) and Meetily (Rust + Tauri on Linux).

Visuals use full-frame desaturated stock footage, lower-third serif captions, archival dossiers, terminal builds, and key number slams on black strictly timed to timeline sections and downbeats.

All text elements respect TikTok safe zones, font sizes remain 34px or greater with proper Vietnamese typography, and outro branding reinforces the MIT open-source focus without watermark chrome.
