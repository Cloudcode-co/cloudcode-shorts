# rvoip — What-if simulation

A cinematic what-if test flight through the rvoip benchmark: the world is full-bleed Pixabay footage (server racks, code, fibre, the AI operator room, a phone box in the rain, the load-monitor trace), always pushing, drifting and shaking harder as the call rate climbs. One live telemetry counter runs the whole film — CPS from 0 to 8,348, down to Asterisk's 150, through 1,204 CPS / 12,000 concurrent calls and a red 2,408 CPS / 503 overload, then down to one local call and back up to 8,348 for the finish — with a chapter title card before each musical section (GIẢ THUYẾT, PHÉP THỬ, HỢP NHẤT, CHẠY THỬ), Playfair italic Vietnamese subtitles low in frame, a vignette, mood grades (cold blue, warm haze, red failure, violet drop) and imagery credited small in a corner.

QA fixes from the rendered stills: reduced and safe-padded the chapter titles so "GIẢ THUYẾT" no longer ran under the TikTok buttons; copied the kit terminal and changed only its wrap rule so the clone/cargo commands break at spaces and slashes instead of slicing "rvoip" or "example"; aligned the 150-CPS drop to the visual cut and held 2,408 CPS through the 503 burst before a quick recovery; shifted the red shot's hue so the monitor trace turns red, not just the wash; darkened the busiest code/photo/laptop plates for HUD contrast; moved the first subtitle of each chapter past its title card; and raised the corner credits to the 34px minimum.
